# リスくんと ひとつのどんぐり — 共通ビジュアル仕様

Create a 16:9 children's picture-book illustration. The generated image must contain
the illustration only; add story text on the Flutter slide, never in the image.

## Canonical style

- warm watercolor and colored-pencil texture
- soft, hand-drawn linework; never a heavy digital outline
- tactile paper picture-book feeling
- bright, gentle, child-friendly atmosphere
- warm golden sunlight, bright dappled light, and soft natural colors
- detailed but not photorealistic
- preserve the established squirrel, rabbit, and world designs from the canonical references
- use depth, atmospheric perspective, and layers of foliage without making the forest dark

## Canonical protagonist: リスくん

- the exact same character as the canonical cover and page 01 references
- warm brown to bright orange fur
- cream-white belly and muzzle
- faint rosy cheeks
- small black eyes and a round, friendly face
- large, rounded, curled tail with stripes
- red neckerchief/bandana
- cute picture-book proportions; normally no clothes other than the red neckerchief

Do not redesign, age, humanize, or replace the protagonist. Do not add a hat,
glasses, shirt, backpack, or other new costume.

## Canonical rabbit: うさぎさん

When the rabbit appears, preserve the rabbit from the canonical page 01 reference:

- white fur
- long ears with pale pink inner ears
- small black eyes and faint rosy cheeks
- blue patterned scarf
- gentle, friendly, peer-like presence

The rabbit is a friend or colleague, not a teacher, boss, or superior.

## Other characters

The owl professor from the previous story is forbidden in this story. A bear or
other small woodland friends may appear only when the storyboard calls for them,
and must look like peers rather than authorities.

## Scale and environment

For close and medium woodland scenes, characters may occupy about half of the
image height. Do not shrink the characters merely to make the forest look large.
Instead, move the camera close to a small part of an enormous forest: foreground
trunks may extend outside the frame and show only a lower trunk or part of the
roots. The implied real-world tree-to-squirrel scale should feel about 10–15:1.

The forest should be bright, with yellow-gold sunlight and soft haze. PAGE 02–04
are still an open meadow or young-tree setting, not a finished forest. PAGE 05 is
the first strong wide reveal of the large forest and community. PAGE 11 shows the
meadow matured into a rich forest.

## Composition rules

Every requested variant must have a visibly different camera structure. Vary
rule-of-thirds placement, side or rear view, high or low angle, foreground
framing, diagonal lines, depth layers, focal point, and negative space. Never
repeat a generic centered two-character pose across all variants or consecutive
pages. Follow the page-specific composition exactly, while keeping the requested
story beat legible at a glance.

Keep the illustration free of logos, watermarks, UI, captions, speech bubbles,
letters, numbers, Japanese text, and story body text. Do not copy the previous
page's framing or composition even when using it as a continuity reference.
